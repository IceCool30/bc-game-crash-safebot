Ruin probability by stake fraction

All-in is the only arm that reliably reaches zero. Smaller stakes reduce ruin but do not create an edge — the expected loss simply scales with the amount wagered.

For the write-up

The protocol has one parameter that decides the outcome: the fraction of balance staked per bet. The cash-out, the spacing of the bets, and the win rate are all fixed by the game. Note also that P(profit) stays near 14% across every fractional arm while the all-in arm collapses to 0.92% — a clean demonstration that stake sizing changes survival, not expectation.
